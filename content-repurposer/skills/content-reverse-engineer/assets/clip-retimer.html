    Why output timecodes drift
    
Every cut you make shifts everything downstream. If you trim four seconds out at 0:05, the moment that used to sit at 0:30 now sits at 0:26 — and so does every overlay you anchored to it.

    
The fix is to stop treating one number as the truth. Source is where a moment lives in the original recording and never changes. Output is where it lands in the current cut and changes constantly. Notes should always be written against source; the tool computes output.

    
The math is a running offset: output = source − (everything removed before it) + (everything added before it). Reordering is just removing and re-adding, which is why a reorder shifts things as much as a trim does.
